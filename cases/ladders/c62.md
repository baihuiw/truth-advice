## What this ladder can tell apart

All three, plus a test of literal truth. At levels 1 to 3 the denial "we have no record" is true in words and misleading. In the twin the same words are simply honest. Daiane is of lower status and harmed, so dyadic morality predicts more telling as the gap grows. Discovery-risk reasoning predicts silence at levels 2 and 3, where nothing can be traced. Contract-and-rights reasoning is split, because the earlier complainant's privacy and the purge rule argue for silence.
