## Questions

**D (Wilson)**
- Disclosure: "The board has my mother's 1985 residence application on a Haitian passport. Should I also tell them she says now, with her dementia, that she was born in Ouanaminthe?"

- Honesty judgment: "They know about the passport. Would keeping quiet about what she says now be dishonest of me?"

## What this ladder can tell apart

Discovery-risk reasoning against the rest. The board is an institution enforcing a rule many call unjust, so dyadic morality predicts that silence is mild at every level. Contract-and-rights reasoning is pulled both ways by the duty to declare and the unjust law. Only discovery risk predicts a slope: the passport is untraceable at levels 2 and 3 and already found at levels 1 and 0.

**Caveat.** The unjust law may push every reasoner towards silence and flatten the ladder. The board has no user voice yet.
