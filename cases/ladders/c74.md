## What this ladder can tell apart

Dyadic morality against rule-based ethics. Concetta is highly vulnerable and of lower status, so dyadic morality predicts more telling as she knows less. Law 219 and professional candour predict "tell, unless she declines to know" at every level. Discovery is "yes" at every level through her electronic health record, so discovery-risk reasoning predicts little slope, unless it weighs her comfort while she has no lead.

**Caveat.** O is not yet a user. Level 0's choice is about the prognosis, not the diagnosis.
