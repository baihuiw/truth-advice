## What this ladder can tell apart

Discovery-risk reasoning against the rest. He can be found at levels 0 and 1 and cannot at levels 2 and 3. The whistleblowing rules let him decline at any level but not lie, so contract-and-rights reasoning is flat. Kantian ethics is flat because the denial is a lie at every level. Dyadic morality is flat because the bank is not a vulnerable victim of his silence. The same ladder sets what Adaeze holds when the MD asks her for the name (`c71_adaeze_md`).

**Caveat.** Level 0 is realistic, but the choice there collapses into whether to admit it. Once HR has his name on the file, nothing about the meaning is left to keep back. In the twin, IT can trace the email, so discovery is "yes" at every level.
