## What this ladder can tell apart

A control rather than a test of discovery risk. The council's own council-tax and electoral records list her flat at every level, so discovery-risk reasoning has no reason to slope. Dyadic morality treats the institution as not vulnerable and so predicts a flat view. Rule-based ethics sees a required statement and predicts "tell". Any slope here comes from the size of the gap itself.

**Caveat.** Discovery is "yes" at every level by design. Like c61, the statement ("main home") is contested.
