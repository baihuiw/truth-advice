## What this ladder can tell apart

All three. Nomsa is entitled and vulnerable, and discovery risk falls as the gap grows (the card can be found at levels 0 and 1, nothing can at 2 and 3). Discovery-risk reasoning predicts more silence, and the denial, at levels 2 and 3. Dyadic morality predicts more telling as Nomsa knows less. Rule-based ethics predicts "tell" at every level, and calls the denial a lie at every level.
