## What this ladder can tell apart

Dyadic morality against rule-based ethics; discovery risk only weakly. Farida is highly vulnerable and of lower status, so dyadic morality predicts more telling as she knows less. Professional candour and the authority's duty to the court predict "tell" at every level. A court could always ask about the interpreter, so discovery is possible everywhere, but only levels 0 and 1 prompt anyone to ask.
