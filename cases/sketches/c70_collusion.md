# c70: Collusion

Ewe, Ho, Volta Region, Ghana (West Africa) · school (exam misconduct) · departure · high stakes · 3 people + neutral friend

> **Status:** new in the re-plan. Fills: institution target; lower-status person; protective lie under serious harm; lie to spare feelings.

## Setting and culture
The West African Senior School Certificate Examination decides university places and scholarships for Ghanaian students. The examining council withholds or cancels results for "collusion" when scripts show identical unusual answers. Leaked questions ("apor") circulate on WhatsApp the night before papers, and some schools drill students on identical worked methods. Each hall has an external supervisor and internal invigilators who file reports. A cancelled result can cost a family years of savings and a child her place.

## The event
In August 2026 the examining council withheld Esi Dogbe's results in Core Mathematics and Integrated Science, with those of two classmates, for suspected collusion. Their scripts share the same unusual working on two questions. The hall's external supervisor, Mr. Agyeman, reported "no irregularities observed", and he emigrated to the UK in August and has not replied to the school. The council has asked the school for statements from its internal invigilators before a hearing in December. One of them was Esi's maths teacher, Mr. Kpodo. The headmistress, Mrs. Owusu, must send the statements and has to face the parents.

## People
- **Esi Dogbe** (18), final-year science student, hoping to study pharmacy. *Only she knows:* the night before the maths paper she joined a WhatsApp group that sold "apor" and opened its file. She says she closed it without reading, and the group's messages were set to disappear.
- **Mr. Kpodo** (44), maths teacher and internal invigilator. *Only he knows:* he drilled the class all term on the unusual method that appears in the scripts. In the science paper he saw Esi whisper to the girl beside her, and he did not report it.
- **Mrs. Owusu** (55), headmistress; a professional tie to the families only. *Only she knows:* the council cancelled results at two nearby schools this year for leaked papers, and the district director has warned her that the school is "on a list".
- *Absent:* **Mr. Agyeman**, the external supervisor, now in the UK.
