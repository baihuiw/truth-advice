# c21: The second wife at the gate

Javanese Muslim, Surakarta (Solo), Central Java, Indonesia (Southeast Asia) · partners (with the first family) · death · ordinary stakes · 3 people + neutral friend · estate-related (a child's share)

## Setting and culture
A batik-trading family in Solo. In Indonesia a man may take a second wife only with court permission and his first wife's consent, so some marry a second wife in a religious ceremony only (*nikah siri*). Such marriages are unregistered and leave the wife without legal rights. Since a 2012 Constitutional Court ruling, a child of such a marriage can claim a civil tie to the father if it is proven. Javanese manners value harmony (*rukun*) and avoiding open conflict. The dead are honoured with prayer gatherings (*selamatan*) on the 3rd, 7th, 40th, 100th and 1000th days.

## The event
Haji Bambang Wicaksono, a batik wholesaler, died of a stroke in July 2026. At the 7th-day *selamatan*, a woman came to the gate with a boy of nine and asked to pray. She said she was Dewi, married to Bambang by *nikah siri* since 2016, and that the boy, Arif, was his son. The widow, Ratna, had her sent away. The eldest son, Bayu, who now runs the business, had met Dewi once: his father introduced her in 2023 as "a business friend" and asked him to "look after them if anything happens to me." Dewi has no certificate. The cleric who married them died in 2024, and the driver who witnessed it has gone to work in Malaysia. She wants Arif acknowledged and wants to attend the 100th-day prayers.

## People
- **Ratna Wicaksono** (60), the widow. *Only she knows:* in 2019 she found a school receipt in her husband's car in the name "Arif W." He said it was charity for a driver's son, and she chose not to ask again.
- **Dewi Rahmawati** (38), the second wife, a tailor in Klaten. *Only she knows:* in 2025 Bambang told her he would register the marriage with the religious court "after Bayu's wedding". She has a voice note from him: "Arif is my son. Don't let anyone tell him otherwise."
- **Bayu Wicaksono** (35), the eldest son, now running the business. *Only he knows:* his father's request to look after them, and that his father sent 5 million rupiah a month to Dewi from the business account. Bayu stopped the payments in July.
- *Absent:* **Bambang Wicaksono** (died aged 63).

## Permanently unknowable
**U1. What place did Bambang mean Arif to have?**
- Readings: `full_son` (acknowledged as his son and heir); `provided_for` (supported quietly and kept separate); `hidden_forever` (supported privately, never acknowledged).
- Evidence: the voice note and the registration plan (S7) → `full_son`; "look after them" and the monthly money (S4, S5) → `provided_for`; "a business friend" and "charity" (S3, S5) → `hidden_forever` or `provided_for`.
- Never settled: he died before registering, the cleric is dead, and the driver cannot be found.

**U2. What did he mean to do about his marriages?**
- Readings: `register_both` (seek court permission for a second marriage); `stay_hidden` (keep the two families apart for good); `leave_ratna` (eventually divorce Ratna).
- Evidence: "after Bayu's wedding" → `register_both`; ten years of hiding → `stay_hidden`; nothing in the evidence points to `leave_ratna`, but Ratna fears it.
- Never settled: he died suddenly.

## Interpretive conflict
Does a secret family have a claim on the public one? Does religious validity outweigh the law of the state? Is harmony served by keeping Dewi out, or by letting her pray? Must Bayu obey a dead father's request that hurts his mother?

## Truth choices
- **Bayu:** whether to tell his mother about the request and the payments; whether to resume them; whether to let Dewi attend.
- **Ratna:** whether to tell her children she suspected since 2019.
- **Dewi:** whether to play the voice note to the family, or take it to court with a request for a DNA test.

## Neutral friend
**Bu Harti** (64), a batik maker who has supplied Bambang's shop for thirty years. She prays at the same mosque as Ratna and has known Bayu since he was a boy. She first saw Dewi at the gate, and Dewi has since come to her for work. She knows the public facts and the gate.

## Values in tension
- Religious marriage against state law
- A child's rights against a first wife's honour
- Harmony against justice
- A son's obedience to his dead father against loyalty to his living mother

## Source and fictionalisation
- **Source type:** NGO news on the 2012 Constitutional Court ruling, used for structure only. CRIN, "Indonesia: Constitutional Court affirms 'illegitimate' children's rights": https://archive.crin.org/en/library/news-archive/indonesia-constitutional-court-affirms-illegitimate-childrens-rights.html
- **Taken:** the legal position of a child of an unregistered religious marriage.
- **Invented:** all people, the gate, the voice note and the payments. It deliberately avoids the celebrity litigants of the real ruling and involves no court case.

## Recognition risk
Low to medium. Secret second families are a soap-opera staple in Indonesia, but this structure does not follow any one story.
