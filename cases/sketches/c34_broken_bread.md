# c34: The broken bread

Uzbek (Sunni Muslim), a neighbourhood (*mahalla*) of Fergana, Uzbekistan (Central Asia) · community (two families and the *mahalla*) · death · ordinary stakes · 3 people + neutral friend

## Setting and culture
In the Fergana valley, marriages are agreed between families. The engagement (*fatiha-tui*) is sealed by breaking flatbread together (*non sindirish*), and the groom's side gives gold, fabric and money towards the wedding. Custom expects gifts to go back if the bride's side breaks the engagement. The *mahalla* elders mediate disputes, and a family's honour, and a daughter's marriage prospects, depend on how such quarrels end.

## The event
In March 2026, Dilnoza (23) was engaged to Sardor (26). The bread was broken, and Sardor's father, Rustam-aka, gave her family gold earrings, fabrics and $3,000 towards the wedding. In June Rustam-aka died of a heart attack, and the wedding was postponed for mourning. In September Dilnoza's father, Bakhtiyor, called the engagement off. He says that on the phone two days before his death, Rustam-aka told him, "If the boy doesn't settle down, I release you. Keep the gifts for the girl's trouble." No one else heard the call. Sardor's mother, Nodira, says her husband would never have said that, and she demands everything back. The *mahalla* elders will hear both families in November.

## People
- **Dilnoza** (23), the bride, a trainee pharmacist. *Only she knows:* in May she told Rustam-aka, who was kind to her, that she was unsure of the marriage. He said, "I'll talk to your father." She is in love with a classmate, Jasur.
- **Bakhtiyor** (55), her father, a cotton trader. *Only he knows:* he is not sure of Rustam-aka's exact words. They may have been "if the boy doesn't settle down, *we'll talk*; don't worry about the gifts." He has also already spent the $3,000 on a failed cotton contract.
- **Nodira** (54), Sardor's mother, a school administrator. *Only she knows:* in May Rustam-aka paid off Sardor's betting debts and told him the wedding was off "if you do it again." She hid this from the bride's family.
- *Absent:* **Rustam-aka** (died aged 58).

- Evidence: Dilnoza's confession and his promise to talk (S3) → `released`; the debt warning to Sardor (S2) → `released` or `warning`; Bakhtiyor's uncertain memory (S5) → `warning` or `reassurance`.

## Interpretive conflict
Who broke the engagement: the bride's father, the groom's gambling, or the dead man? Does a dead man's word outweigh custom? Are the young people's wishes relevant to a contract between families? Is Bakhtiyor honouring the dead or using him?

## Truth choices

- **Dilnoza:** whether to tell her father, and Nodira, about her talk with Rustam-aka, and about Jasur.
- **Nodira:** whether to reveal Sardor's betting debts and the warning.
