# c44: Two shots

Finnish (Lutheran, rural), a moose-hunting club in North Karelia, Finland (Northern Europe) · community (hunting club) · death · high stakes · 4 people + neutral friend

> **Status:** keep.

## Setting and culture
In the forests of eastern Finland, the autumn moose hunt is the high point of village life. The club drives game towards a line of standers at fixed posts, each with a permitted shooting sector. A hunting leader sets the posts and the safety brief. Accidents are investigated by the police as possible manslaughter by negligence. A club is a brotherhood with a strong code of not talking, and everyone knows everyone.

## The event
On 10 October 2026 the club drove a moose towards the line. As it crossed between posts 4 and 5, two standers fired: Esa Kinnunen from post 4 and Mikko Hyvönen from post 5. The moose escaped, wounded. Pekka Rautio, at post 6, was hit by one bullet and died. The bullet passed through him and was never found in the marsh, and both rifles are the same calibre. The police cannot tell whose shot killed him, and both men are under investigation. Pekka's widow, Sari, wants to know. The hunting leader, Jari, has told the police that the posts were set "as usual."

## People
- **Esa Kinnunen** (63), stander at post 4, a retired forestry worker. *Only he knows:* as he pulled the trigger he saw a flash of orange in the trees. He has told the police he saw only the moose. He is thinking of taking the blame outright, to spare Mikko, who has two small children.
- **Mikko Hyvönen** (31), stander at post 5, an electrician. *Only he knows:* he drank two beers with lunch at the club hut, against club rules, and he fired as the moose reached the very edge of his sector, perhaps beyond it.
- **Sari Rautio** (56), Pekka's widow, a nurse. *Only she knows:* the night before, Pekka told her, "Jari wants me at a new post tomorrow. I don't like it; it's in the open."
- **Jari Lehtonen** (60), the hunting leader. *Only he knows:* that morning he moved post 6 forward without changing the sectors for posts 4 and 5, and told only Pekka.
- *Absent:* **Pekka Rautio** (died aged 58).

## Interpretive conflict
