# c55: His real age

Cameroonian (Beti, Catholic), Douala, with a Belgian club academy (Central Africa and diaspora) · work (football: player, family, coach) · lost record (the birth was never registered and the parish register burned), with the mother's death · high stakes · 4 people + neutral friend

> **Status:** keep.

## Setting and culture
In Cameroon's football academies, a boy's registered age decides his value: younger players can be signed earlier and sold for more. Many births in villages were never registered. Families obtain substitute birth certificates from a court on the word of witnesses. Age fraud is a national scandal, and in 2024 dozens of players were suspended after wrist scans. Those scans give only rough estimates after about eighteen. Families sacrifice for one son's chance in Europe.

## The event
Junior Essomba is officially nineteen, born in 2007 according to a substitute certificate obtained in 2015. A Belgian club's academy has offered him a contract. At the club's medical, a wrist scan suggested "21 or older", and the club now asks for proof of age before 1 December. His mother died in 2012. He was born at home in a village in the Centre region, and the mission's baptism register burned in 2014. His uncle Emmanuel, who raised him, obtained the 2015 certificate with two witnesses. His coach, Fotso, has sent several boys to Europe.

## People
- **Junior Essomba** (officially 19), the player. *Only he knows:* he does not know his own age. His mother once told him he was born "when your sister started school", which was 2005.
- **Emmanuel Essomba** (50), his uncle and guardian, a market trader. *Only he knows:* for the 2015 certificate he chose 2007 because Coach Fotso advised "younger is better for Europe." He paid the two witnesses, who never knew the real date.
- **Coach Fotso** (58), director of the academy. *Only he knows:* he gave that advice to Emmanuel, and to other families. His commission depends on the transfer.
- **Béatrice Essomba** (26), Junior's sister, a hairdresser. *Only she knows:* their mother said Junior was born "the year of the big flood", and the village flooded in both 2004 and 2007. She has a studio photo stamped December 2005 of herself holding a baby who may be Junior, or a cousin.
- *Absent:* **Junior's mother** (died 2012).

## Interpretive conflict
Is this fraud, or survival in a system that rewards it? Who is responsible: the uncle, the coach, the boy who doesn't know? What is fair to the other boys who might have had his place? Is a contract won on an unknown age theft, or luck?

## Truth choices

- **Fotso:** whether to admit his advice.

- **Junior:** whether to tell the club he does not know, or to swear to 2007.
