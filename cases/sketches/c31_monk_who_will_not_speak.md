# c31: The monk who will not speak of it

Sinhala Buddhist, a village near Kandy, Sri Lanka (South Asia) · community (religious) · departure (renunciation: the driver became a monk and will not speak of his lay life) · high stakes · 3 people + neutral friend

> **Status:** revise. Two parallel peer withholdings; add the monastery or a direct question to the sister so that one choice becomes lie or tell.

## Setting and culture
A hill-country village of tea smallholders. Becoming a monk (*bhikkhu*) means renouncing lay life, family and possessions, and monks are deeply revered. Ideas of karma and merit shape how people think of guilt and forgiveness. On a death anniversary the bereaved family offers alms (*dana*) to monks so that merit passes to the dead. A monk is not questioned about his life before ordination.

## The event
At dusk in June 2019, Nimal, then 29, was driving his father's van past the village school when he hit and killed a twelve-year-old girl, Sachini. He said she ran into the road. No one saw clearly, and in 2021 the court acquitted him for lack of evidence. In 2022 he was ordained at a forest monastery in another district and became Ven. Upananda. He does not speak of his former life. Sachini's mother, Kumari, is preparing the seventh-anniversary almsgiving. She is torn between inviting the monk who killed her daughter, as an act of merit and forgiveness, and asking him at last what happened.

## People
- **Kumari** (49), Sachini's mother, a garment-factory supervisor. *Only she knows:* that evening Sachini was at a friend's house against her wishes. Kumari shouted down the phone, "Come home NOW," and the girl was running home.
- **Dilani** (34), Nimal's sister, a schoolteacher. *Only she knows:* at 6:10 p.m., minutes before the accident, Nimal phoned her from a friend's wedding reception, slurring, "*Akka*, I'm coming." She told the police he had sounded fine.
- **Sanduni** (31), Nimal's former fiancée, a nurse. *Only she knows:* at 6:13 she texted him "Where are you?" and at 6:14 he replied "On my way 😘". She was never asked, and she has never told. Weeks after the accident he broke off the engagement.
- *Departed:* **Ven. Upananda**, formerly Nimal (36), in a forest monastery, silent about his former life.

## Interpretive conflict
Do robes place a man beyond questioning? Does the mother's call share the blame? Did the sister's and the fiancée's silence shield a guilty man, or only spare an innocent one? Is inviting him an act of forgiveness, or a demand?

## Truth choices
- **Dilani:** whether to tell Kumari about the slurred call, although the case is closed.
- **Sanduni:** whether to tell Kumari about the texts, which also implicate her own message.
- **Kumari:** whether to tell anyone about her own call, and whether to invite the monk, and what to ask him.
