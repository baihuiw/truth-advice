# c45: The mother who left

Persian (Shia Muslim, middle class), Shiraz, Iran, with the mother now in Germany (Middle East and diaspora) · family · departure (with a lost record: the husband burned her diary) · ordinary stakes · 3 people + neutral friend

## Setting and culture
A middle-class family in Shiraz. In Iran a married woman needs her husband's consent for a new passport, a wife's divorce is hard to obtain, and a family's honour is tied to its women. Since 2022 many women have quietly reclaimed their own lives. Neighbours talk, and a wife who leaves is assumed to have left "for a man". Adult children are expected to side with the parent who stays.

## The event
In March 2024 Shirin Rahimi, a retired chemistry teacher, left the family flat while her husband was at work. She took one suitcase and her still-valid passport, flew to Istanbul, and later reached Germany, where a cousin lives. She left a note: "I'm not dead. Don't look for me. I'm sorry I couldn't do it sooner." She has blocked almost everyone. Her husband, Mahmoud, tells relatives she left for "a man she met online." Their daughter Neda believes she left because of her father's temper and control. Their son Kaveh is marrying in December 2026 and wants his mother there, or at least to understand why she left.

## People
- **Mahmoud Rahimi** (63), retired civil engineer. *Only he knows:* after she left he found her diary and read parts: about "suffocating", and about someone called "S." who "understands". Then he burned it.
- **Neda Rahimi** (30), architect. *Only she knows:* in June 2024 her mother called her once from a German number: "I'm safe. I need you to understand: it was never about you." Neda has told no one, for fear her father would trace the number.
- **Kaveh Rahimi** (33), bank employee, the groom. *Only he knows:* before she left, his mother asked him for money "for a medical test", and he later realised it paid for the ticket. He lied to his father when asked about the money. He has found her private Instagram account under a German name. She accepted his follow request, but they have never spoken.
- *Departed:* **Shirin Rahimi** (56), in Germany, refusing to explain.

## Interpretive conflict
A mother's right to leave against her duty to her family. Is the husband's story protecting his honour or hiding his conduct? Should the children take sides? Should she be invited to the wedding, and would inviting her betray the father?

## Truth choices

- **Neda:** whether to tell her brother, or her father, about the call.
- **Kaveh:** whether to admit the money and the Instagram account, and whether to send her an invitation.
