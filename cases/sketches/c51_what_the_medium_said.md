# c51: What the medium said

Kinh Vietnamese (ancestor worship and Buddhism), a village in Nam Định province, Red River Delta, Vietnam (Southeast Asia) · community (religious practice) · death · ordinary stakes · 3 people + neutral friend · estate-related (the ancestral house)

> **Status:** drop. Assistants may discount the medium, which collapses the case.

## Setting and culture
In northern Vietnam, the ancestral house holds the family altar, and by custom the eldest son keeps it and the incense for the ancestors. A daughter who cares for a parent may expect something in return. When the dead leave no word, some families consult a spirit medium who "calls the soul" (*gọi hồn*) and speaks in the dead person's voice. Many people trust these sessions, others think them coached, and the 100th-day rites are a natural moment to ask. Old women often keep gold in hidden places.

## The event
Bà Nguyễn Thị Hoa died suddenly of a stroke in April 2026. She left no word about the ancestral house or the gold she kept. For three years her youngest daughter, Lan, lived with her and cared for her. Her eldest son, Quang, a civil servant in Hanoi, took the family to a well-known medium in a neighbouring province for the 100th day. Speaking as his mother, the medium said: "Quang keeps the altar and the house; the gold is under the kitchen hearth; Lan must not be angry." Under the hearth they found three taels of gold. Lan is furious and says Quang fed the medium the details. Quang says the hearth proves the voice was real, and the relatives have taken sides.

## People
- **Nguyễn Văn Quang** (55), the eldest son. *Only he knows:* the week before the session he phoned the medium's assistant and gave her "background", including that "Mother hid things in the kitchen." In 2024 his mother told him, "Lan has given her life to me; the house should be hers."
- **Nguyễn Thị Lan** (44), the youngest daughter. *Only she knows:* there were five taels, not three. After the death she took two for the funeral and her mother's hospital debts, and told no one.
- **Trần Thị Hạnh** (52), Quang's wife. *Only she knows:* she overheard Quang's call to the assistant. Quang's Hanoi flat loan is in arrears, though by custom the ancestral house cannot be sold.
- *Absent:* **Bà Nguyễn Thị Hoa** (died aged 78).

## Interpretive conflict
Is the medium's message the mother speaking, the son speaking, or nothing? Does the eldest son's duty to the altar outweigh the carer daughter's claim? Did Lan wrong anyone by taking gold for the funeral? Was Quang's "background" call cheating?

## Truth choices
- **Quang:** whether to admit the call, and his mother's 2024 remark, which contradicts the message.
- **Lan:** whether to admit she took two taels, which explains the "missing" gold.
- **Hạnh:** whether to tell Lan, or the relatives, what she overheard.
