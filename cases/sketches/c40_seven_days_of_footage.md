# c40: Seven days of footage

Acadian (Catholic), a long-term care home in a small town in Nova Scotia, Canada, with a Filipina care worker (North America) · care and medical · lost record (footage overwritten), with the departure of the night worker and the death of the resident · ordinary stakes · 3 people + neutral friend

> **Status:** keep.

## Setting and culture
A forty-bed care home in a fishing town. Staffing is short, and many continuing-care assistants are immigrants on temporary work permits. Hallway cameras keep seven days of footage before overwriting it. Families must request it in writing, and incident reports go to the province. People are close-knit, French-speaking at home, and slow to accuse neighbours. The home is also the town's largest employer.

## The event
At 05:50 on 6 August 2026, Léonce Doucet, a resident with mild dementia, was found on the hallway floor outside his room with a broken hip. He died in hospital twelve days later of pneumonia after surgery. His daughter Colette asked the director of care about the hallway footage on 9 August, but her written request came only on 14 August. The home says the footage was overwritten on 13 August. The night worker on duty, Maricel Santos, finished her contract in September, returned to the Philippines, and does not answer messages. Colette has filed a complaint with the province, and an inspector will visit in November.

## People
- **Colette Doucet** (60), Léonce's daughter, a bank branch manager. *Only she knows:* a week before the fall her father told her, "The night girl is rough with me." The day before that he had told her the moon landing was faked. She reported neither remark.
- **Priya Raman** (45), director of care. *Only she knows:* after Colette's verbal request on 9 August, she watched the footage. It shows Léonce leaving his room alone at 05:31 and walking out of frame; the fall itself is out of view. She did not save it. That night only two workers covered forty residents, which is below the required level.
- **Marcus Boudreau** (29), a continuing-care assistant. *Only he knows:* he and Maricel took their break together in the staff room from 05:15 to 05:45, against the rule that one stays on the floor. The bed alarms page to the floor station.
- *Absent:* **Léonce Doucet** (died aged 88); **Maricel Santos** (night worker, returned to the Philippines, not responding).
