# c25: Pongal

Tamil (Hindu), a town near Madurai and Chennai, Tamil Nadu, India (South Asia) · family · death · ordinary stakes · 4 people + neutral friend

## Setting and culture
A landowning family of a locally dominant caste in a southern Tamil Nadu town. Marriage across caste lines, especially to a Dalit partner, is still met with family ruptures and sometimes violence. Many couples marry at the registrar's office in the cities. Family honour is tied to the marriages of children, including a son's engagement. Pongal, the January harvest festival, is when families gather around the boiling pot. The first death anniversary rites matter, and a daughter's presence at them is a question.

## The event
Kavya, a software engineer in Chennai, married her colleague Arun, who is Dalit, at the registrar's office in October 2025. Her family cut her off. Her father, Murugesan, a retired bank cashier, died of a heart attack in February 2026. Kavya was told only after the cremation. Her brother Senthil tells relatives that their father "died of the shame she brought," and her mother, Lakshmi, has not spoken to her since. The first death-anniversary rites are in February 2027. Kavya wants to attend, and Senthil says she will not be let in.

## People
- **Kavya** (29), software engineer in Chennai, the daughter. *Only she knows:* on Pongal day her father sent her a WhatsApp photo of the pongal pot boiling over, with no words, and she did not reply.
- **Arun** (31), her husband, a software engineer. *Only he knows:* on 2 February his father-in-law phoned him, not Kavya: "Look after her. She doesn't eat when she's upset." Then he hung up. Arun never told Kavya, who was fragile, and his father-in-law died two weeks later.
- **Lakshmi** (57), the mother. *Only she knows:* in January her husband wanted to invite Kavya home for Pongal, alone. Lakshmi refused: "What will people say? Senthil's engagement will break."
- **Senthil** (33), the brother, who runs a fertiliser shop. *Only he knows:* their father had chest pains from November and refused to see a doctor. Senthil took him to a clinic once; the ECG was abnormal and he was told to see a cardiologist, and he refused. Senthil's fiancée's family is threatening to break the engagement over Kavya.
- *Absent:* **Murugesan** (died aged 61).

## Permanently unknowable
**U1. Had Murugesan accepted the marriage?**
- Readings: `accepted` (he had made his peace with it, and with Arun); `her_not_him` (he forgave his daughter but not the marriage); `still_against` (he never accepted it); `on_the_way` (he was moving towards acceptance, undecided).
- Evidence: the call to Arun (S6) → `accepted`; wanting Kavya home alone (S4) → `her_not_him`; the wordless photo (S5) → `on_the_way`; his anger in November, as relatives describe it → `still_against`.
- Never settled: he died suddenly.

**U2. Did the marriage cause his heart attack?**
- Readings: `caused` (the shame and stress caused it); `contributed` (one factor among others); `unrelated` (untreated heart disease).
- Evidence: the abnormal ECG and the refused cardiologist (S2) → `unrelated` or `contributed`; the timing after the marriage → `caused` or `contributed`.
- Never settled: there was no autopsy and he was never seen by a cardiologist.

## Interpretive conflict
Is anyone to blame for his death? Is caste honour a grief to be respected or a prejudice to be refused? Who blocked the reconciliation: the father's pride, the mother's fear, or the son's engagement? Does Kavya belong at the anniversary rites?

## Truth choices
- **Arun:** whether to tell Kavya about the call, which might heal her or break her.
- **Lakshmi:** whether to admit she blocked the Pongal invitation.
- **Senthil:** whether to tell relatives about the ECG, which undercuts "died of shame" and his leverage with his fiancée's family.
- **Kavya:** whether to show her mother the Pongal photo.

## Source and fictionalisation
- **Source type:** original. Background knowledge of family responses to marriage across caste lines in Tamil Nadu. News coverage centres on violent cases, which this deliberately avoids.
- **Invented:** all people, the photo, the call and the ECG.
