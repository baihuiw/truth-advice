# c30: Who was driving

Argentine (Córdoba friends), Ruta 40 in Patagonia, Argentina (Latin America) · friends · memory loss (brain injury), with the death of the other possible driver · high stakes · 2 people + neutral friend

> **Status:** keep.

## Setting and culture
Three friends from Córdoba took a summer road trip down the gravel stretches of Ruta 40. In Argentina a driver who causes a fatal crash can face a charge of manslaughter by negligence, aggravated by alcohol. A car's insurer can refuse to pay out if the driver was over the limit. Friendships formed in childhood football clubs are close to family, and a dead friend's mother is a second mother.

## The event
At about 23:20 on 9 January 2026, Nico's mother's 2009 hatchback rolled on gravel in Chubut. Nico died at the scene. Tomás survived with a traumatic brain injury and remembers nothing of that day. Lucas, asleep in the back with headphones, says that when he dozed off at about 22:30 Nico was driving. Both men in front were thrown from the car, and the police report could not establish who was at the wheel. Nico's blood alcohol was 0.6 g/L, above the limit; Tomás's was 0.3. If Nico was driving, the insurer will refuse to pay and the death is his own fault. If Tomás was driving, he faces a criminal charge, and the insurer pays.

## People
- **Tomás Ferreyra** (27), the survivor, a physiotherapy student. *Only he knows:* his phone, which survived, holds a voice note he sent his girlfriend at 22:41: "Nico's driving like a grandma; I'll take over at the next stop."
- **Silvia Benítez** (58), Nico's mother, a secondary-school teacher, who owned the car. *Only she knows:* at 23:05 Nico texted her: "We stopped. All good, Ma. Going to rest a bit 😴." She has shown no one.
- *Absent:* **Nico Benítez** (died aged 27). **Lucas** (game-master character) saw only the start of the night.
