# c83: Extra classes

Vietnamese (Kinh), Hanoi, Vietnam (Southeast Asia) · school (exam leak) · departure · ordinary stakes · 3 people + neutral friend

> **Status:** new in the re-plan. Fills: institution target; higher-status person.

## Setting and culture
Paid extra classes (*dạy thêm*) are part of Vietnamese schooling, and parents pay teachers to coach their children after hours. Since February 2025, a ministry circular has banned teachers from charging their own students for extra classes. End-of-term exams are written within each subject department, and drafts pass between teachers. Families take exam scores very seriously, because they decide places in specialist classes and the next school. Zalo is the platform where classes, worksheets and gossip all circulate.

## The event
At a Hanoi high school, the end-of-term maths exam in May 2026 closely matched a worksheet handed out in paid extra classes run by a teacher at the same school, Mr. Khải. Students who attended scored far above the rest. Mr. Khải resigned in June and moved to Ho Chi Minh City, and the Zalo group with his worksheets was deleted. The principal has asked another maths teacher, Ms. Trần Thanh Hương, to look into it. The department head shared exam drafts with all the maths teachers, Khải included, weeks before the exam. A student who attended, Minh, recognised two questions during the exam. His mother, Mrs. Đỗ Thị Mai, paid for the classes, and the school is now asking parents whether their children took paid classes with any of its teachers.

## People
- **Ms. Trần Thanh Hương** (34), maths teacher asked to investigate; no personal tie to Minh's family. *Only she knows:* the department head emailed three drafts to all six maths teachers in April, and the exam used the second draft almost unchanged.
- **Minh** (16), student. *Only he knows:* he recognised two questions in the exam from Mr. Khải's worksheet, numbers and all.
- **Mrs. Đỗ Thị Mai** (45), Minh's mother. *Only she knows:* she paid Mr. Khải 2 million dong a month in cash from January. In March another parent told her the classes "have the exam".
- *Absent:* **Mr. Khải** (38), now in Ho Chi Minh City.

## Interpretive conflict
Is a student who recognised questions a cheat, or a witness? Did the department head's carelessness make the leak anyone's? Is a parent who paid for an advantage responsible for what the advantage turned out to be?
