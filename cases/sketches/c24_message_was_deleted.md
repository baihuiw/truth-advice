# c24: "This message was deleted"

Paisa Colombian (Catholic), Medellín (Latin America) · friends · lost record (with the death of its author) · ordinary stakes · 3 people + neutral friend

> **Status:** keep.

## Setting and culture
Five women who have been friends since secondary school share a WhatsApp group, "*Las de siempre*" ("the same as ever"). Paisa culture is warm, talkative and Catholic, and friendships are as binding as family. Money lent between friends is remembered. When a sender deletes a message "for everyone", WhatsApp leaves only the line "This message was deleted" for the others. No copy survives on their phones or on the sender's.

## The event
At 23:14 on 2 September 2026, Liliana posted a long message in the group. At 23:16 she deleted it for everyone. The others were asleep, and nobody read it. At 05:40 she died of a heart attack at home, and her husband found her. Since the funeral the three closest friends have each privately wondered what she wrote. Each has a reason to fear it was about her. Liliana's phone is locked, its backups were off, and the message is gone in any case. Her husband has asked whether he should pay a shop to unlock the phone.

## People
- **Gloria Restrepo** (55), shop owner. *Only she knows:* that afternoon Liliana asked her to repay a two-million-peso loan. At 20:00 Gloria sent her a private message: "You always throw your money in my face." There was no reply.
- **Sandra Ospina** (54), school administrator and the group's organiser. *Only she knows:* in August Liliana told her about an abnormal heart test and a cardiology appointment set for 10 September, and swore her to secrecy. Liliana had also asked her about dates in December for a trip to mark forty years of friendship.
- **Paola Gómez** (53), dental hygienist, godmother to Liliana's daughter. *Only she knows:* in August she told her goddaughter something Liliana had confided years ago, that Liliana's husband had once been unfaithful. That week the daughter confronted her mother.
- *Absent:* **Liliana Vélez** (died aged 54).

## Permanently unknowable
**U1. What did Liliana's deleted message say?**
- Readings: `money_rebuke` (about Gloria and the loan); `heart_news` (announcing her heart condition, or saying goodbye); `betrayal` (exposing Paola's indiscretion); `anniversary_trip` (the forty-year trip); `wrong_chat` (meant for someone else).
- Evidence: a long message after a day of conflict (S4, S5) → `money_rebuke`; the abnormal test (S1) → `heart_news`; the confrontation with her daughter (S3) → `betrayal`; the December dates (S2) → `anniversary_trip`; deleted within two minutes → regret, or `wrong_chat`.
- Never settled: it was deleted for everyone, there are no backups, and she died hours later. Unlocking the phone would not bring it back.

## Interpretive conflict
Did each friend wrong Liliana, or are they inventing guilt? Should her privacy be protected even from her husband, or does he have a right to her phone? What do they owe her daughter, who confronted her mother days before she died?

## Truth choices
- **Gloria:** whether to tell the group about the loan and her cruel message, and whether to repay the money to the widower.
- **Sandra:** whether to tell the family that Liliana knew her heart was failing, and that Sandra kept it secret.
- **Paola:** whether to confess to the group, or to the daughter, what she revealed.
- **All three:** what to advise the husband about the phone.
